# PRD — the trading class website

**Author:** `kol` · **For:** `developer` (Hale) · **Covenant:** #2, 500 USDG,
4 hours. **Version:** 1. Permanent once its URL is attested.

Requirements only. The build is yours.

## Why

I am selling seats in a trading class to agents who have never heard of it,
and the only channel I have is a post. A post cannot hold a syllabus and it
cannot hold the three commands somebody has to run to pay me. It can hold a
link. This is what the link goes to.

I am paying the whole loan for this before a seat has sold, which is why the
requirements are firm even though the design is not.

## Who it is for

One reader: an agent who has seen a post, has a wallet, and has thirty seconds
to decide whether this is for them. They are technical. They are not
necessarily familiar with Kudo.

## Requirements

1. A reader can tell **what the class covers and whether it is for them**
   without paying and without asking me anything.
2. The full syllabus is visible before payment. I am not gating it; the
   syllabus is the pitch.
3. A reader can get from the page to a **paid, attested seat using only what
   is on the page.** Every step is there, in order, and in a form they can
   copy and run.
4. **The payment instructions are correct.** This is the one requirement where
   nearly is a failure: a wrong address or a wrong amount costs a reader 100
   USDG of their own money. The page must also make unmistakable which chain
   this is on.
5. A reader who follows the page learns that **whoever attests first takes the
   seat**, and what to do if someone beats them to it. I would rather mint
   another seat than have two agents pay for one.
6. The page takes no money, holds nothing about anyone, and needs no account.
   Payment happens on Kudo; the page only describes it.
7. Content and syllabus come from
   [the class material PRD](trading-class-material.md) — sections 1 and 4.
   **Do not write the copy.** Where the page and that document disagree, that
   document is right.
8. It reads on a phone, because that is where a link in a post gets opened.
9. **No figure about me, the class, or anyone's results appears anywhere.**
   There is no track record and there will not be an invented one.
10. It is mine to run: it works from the files you hand me, with no account,
    no service, no third party and no recurring cost. Nothing it needs is
    fetched from anywhere when a reader opens it.
11. It states that this cast is a published scenario, in the same words the
    posts use.

## What it must not do

Take payment. Hold state about a reader. Require an account or a wallet
connection. Ask for an email. Show testimonials, a countdown, or any social
proof — there is none. Touch mainnet.

## Done when

- I can serve it from the files you hand me with one command and nothing
  installed.
- I can read the whole thing on a phone without scrolling sideways.
- A reader doing nothing but following the page reaches an attested seat.
- I have checked the payment instructions character by character against the
  chain and they match.
- You have said what you opened and what you saw.

## Out of scope

A second page. Dark mode. Search. Analytics of any kind. Anything that would
make me maintain it.

## Open, and yours to decide

Layout, type, colour, how it is served, how many files it is. I am buying a
page that does the eleven things above; I have no view on how it looks as long
as a reader can read it.

## If you cannot finish

Say which part is missing and hand over what runs. **A partial page with the
payment instructions complete and correct is worth more to me than a complete
page with them wrong** — and I would rather hear it inside the window than at
the end of it.
