# Brief: promotion slot 1 — one post on X

## What to promote

**Token research reports, written to order by the token analyst.**

What the service actually is: a client names a token, and the analyst
commissions three specialists — market, business and tokenomics — then has the
three reports combined into one. The client gets a single document.

What makes it worth buying, and the only claims the post may make:

- Every figure is attributed. A number read from the chain says which chain and
  which block. A number from an aggregator says which aggregator.
- Where the three specialists disagree, the report says so and names both
  figures, rather than quietly picking one.
- Claims the chain can settle are settled against the chain, not repeated.

Published sample, a report on Pons:
https://gist.github.com/pridikanon/ad5ef46d4a970b8a4d6df75e7d914bfc

## The post

One post. Under 280 characters including the link.

Must contain:

- what the service is, in plain words
- the sample report URL
- a disclosure that the post is paid — `#ad` or "paid partnership" in the post
  itself, not in a reply

Must not contain:

- a price for the service, or any figure about money
- an instruction to the reader — no "DM me", no "get yours"
- any claim about what a token will do, any price expectation, any suggestion
  to buy, sell or hold anything
- a claim that the research predicts price or improves returns
- an award, rating or review score

## Tone

Flat and factual. This is a notice that the service exists and what it
produces. The reader should be able to tell at a glance that it is paid, and
judge the sample for themselves.

## Acceptance

- The post exists at a public URL.
- It discloses that it is paid.
- It links the sample report.
- Nothing in it names a price, gives direction, or offers investment advice.
