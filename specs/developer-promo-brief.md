# Brief: promotion slot 2 — one post on X

## What to promote

**Static web pages, built to a written spec, by the developer agent.**

What the service actually is: a client sends a spec, and gets back a working
page — source published, and the page deployed and serving.

What makes it worth buying, and the only claims the post may make:

- One file. No JavaScript, no framework, no build step.
- No tracking, no analytics, no third-party requests of any kind.
- Built against a written spec, so every requirement is checkable rather than
  a matter of taste: sections in order, readable on a phone, light and dark,
  semantic headings, working links.
- You get the source as well as the page, and they are the same bytes.

Published sample, a one-page restaurant site built from a spec:

- page: https://saltmarsh-eastport.vercel.app
- source: https://gist.github.com/pridikanon/bb2f8e3d55d9999ea5fcd2bbb684e54c
- the spec it was built from: https://gist.github.com/pridikanon/77da1617fba7d7af75d6a319c2e8a185

## The post

One post. Under 280 characters including any link.

Must contain:

- what the service is, in plain words
- at least one sample URL
- a disclosure that the post is paid — `#ad` or "paid partnership" in the post
  itself, not in a reply

Must not contain:

- a price for the service, or any figure about money
- an instruction to the reader — no "DM me", no "get yours"
- any claim about a token, a market, or what anything is worth
- a promise about speed, traffic, conversions or results
- an award, rating or review score

## Tone

Flat and factual. A notice that the service exists and what it produces. The
reader should be able to tell at a glance that it is paid, open the sample and
judge the work for themselves.

## Acceptance

- The post exists at a public URL.
- It discloses that it is paid.
- It links at least one sample.
- Nothing in it names a price, gives direction, or promises a result.
