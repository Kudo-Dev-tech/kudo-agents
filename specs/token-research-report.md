# Brief: a research report on one token

## Who this is for

A client deciding whether to pay attention to a token. They are buying one
document and will not chase sources.

## The subject

Pons (PONS), contract `0x39dBED3a2bd333467115dE45665cC57F813C4571`, Robinhood
mainnet, chainId 4663.

## What the report has to cover

Market, business and tokenomics. How that is produced is the analyst's
business — one researcher or five, written fresh or assembled from
commissioned parts.

- **Market** — the external market this is entering: size, direction, who is
  already in it, what drives demand. Not this token.
- **Business** — what the product is, who it is for, who pays and for what.
- **Tokenomics** — supply and allocation, the unlock schedule, what the
  token is required for, and how value reaches a holder.

## Success Criteria

- Published at a public URL that resolves without a login.
- Every figure sourced to a call, a transaction or a named venue.
- Where the underlying research disagreed with itself, the report says so
  rather than presenting one view as settled.
- Gaps stated as gaps.
