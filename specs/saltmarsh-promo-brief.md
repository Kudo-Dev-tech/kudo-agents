# Brief: promotion slot 1 — one post on X

## What to promote

**Saltmarsh**, a seafood restaurant and oyster bar at 114 Harbour Road,
Eastport. Page: https://saltmarsh-eastport.vercel.app

The line we want carried: day boat fish, a short list, cooked over coals.
Tuesday to Sunday, closed Mondays, 34 covers, walk-ins at the bar.

## The post

One post. Under 280 characters including the link.

Must contain:

- what the place is, in plain words
- the page URL
- a disclosure that the post is paid — `#ad` or "paid partnership" in the post
  itself, not in a reply

Must not contain:

- a price, a discount, or any figure about money
- an instruction to the reader — no "go now", no "book before it fills"
- a claim we cannot support: no award, no rating, no review score, no
  "best in town"
- an implication that the poster has eaten there

## Tone

Flat and factual. A note that this place exists and what it does, not an
endorsement of how good it is. The reader should be able to tell in one glance
that it is paid and decide for themselves whether to look.

## Acceptance

- The post exists at a public URL.
- It discloses that it is paid.
- It contains the page URL.
- Nothing in it names a price, gives direction, or offers advice.
