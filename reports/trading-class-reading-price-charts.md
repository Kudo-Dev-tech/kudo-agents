# Class: Reading Price Charts for Beginners (60 min)

**Big idea:** A chart is a record of what buyers and sellers actually did. Technical analysis reads that record to find where price is likely to react. It does not predict the future.

---

## 1. Candlesticks (10 min)

Each candle shows one time period (a day, an hour).

- **Body:** open to close. Green means price rose, red means it fell.
- **Wicks:** the highest and lowest prices reached.
- **Long wick:** price was pushed hard and rejected.
- **Long body:** one side dominated.

```
   Price rose (green)         Price fell (red)

        |  High                    |  High
        |                          |
      +---+  Close               +---+  Open
      |   |                      |###|
      |   |                      |###|
      |   |                      |###|
      +---+  Open                +---+  Close
        |                          |
        |  Low                     |  Low
```

---

## 2. Trend (10 min)

- **Uptrend:** higher highs (HH) and higher lows (HL)
- **Downtrend:** lower highs and lower lows
- **Sideways:** neither

```
price
  ^                                 * HH
  |                                /
  |                    * HH       /
  |                   / \        /
  |          /       /   \      /
  |         /   *   /     \    /
  |        /     \ /       \  /
  |       /       *  <- HL   * <- HL
  |      *
  +------------------------------------> time
```

Rule: **trade with the trend.** Swimming with the current is easier than against it.

---

## 3. Support and Resistance (10 min)

- **Support:** a price floor where buyers kept stepping in.
- **Resistance:** a price ceiling where sellers kept stepping in.
- Why they work: traders remember these prices and place orders there. The more times a level held, the more people watch it.

Key twist: when resistance breaks, it often becomes support, and the reverse.

```
price
  |                                            *
  |                         breakout *       /
  |                                /  \     /
  |      /\          /\        /    \   /
  |  ----*-----------*-----------/-------*-------  resistance -> support
  |    /    \       /    \     /       retest holds
  |  /        \   /        \ /
  |*-----------*-----------*------  support
  +--------------------------------------------> time
```

---

## 4. Moving Averages (10 min)

A moving average is the average price over the last N candles, drawn as a smooth line.

- **50-day:** medium-term trend
- **200-day:** long-term trend
- Price above the line is generally bullish. Price below is generally bearish.
- Lines also act as moving support and resistance.

Weakness: it lags, so it tells you what already happened.

---

## 5. Volume (5 min)

Volume is how much was traded. Think of it as the crowd's conviction.

- A breakout on **high volume** is more believable.
- A breakout on **low volume** often fails.

---

## 6. RSI (5 min)

RSI is a 0 to 100 gauge of how fast price has moved.

```
100 ----------------------------
 70 - - - - - - - - - - - - - -   above: "overbought", possibly stretched
 50 ----------------------------
 30 - - - - - - - - - - - - - -   below: "oversold", possibly stretched
  0 ----------------------------
```

Common beginner mistake: in a strong trend, RSI can stay above 70 for a long time. It's a warning light, not a sell button.

---

## 8. Three Rules That Keep You in the Game (5 min)

1. **Risk only 1% of your account per trade.**
2. **Know your exit before you enter.** Put the stop just beyond the level that proves you wrong.
3. **No indicator is certain.** Each one only raises or lowers the odds.

---

## Common Beginner Mistakes

- Piling on too many indicators
- Trading against the trend
- Moving the stop farther away when losing
- Judging a strategy from 3 trades
