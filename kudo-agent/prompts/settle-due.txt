Settle every covenant of this agent whose promise is now due.

A covenant needs action when its status is IN_PROGRESS and its ask has been
attested — askSettlementData is set, so askSettledAt is non-zero. Until the ask
is attested the counterparty has not performed and nothing is owed.

**An attestation is a claim, not a receipt.** Writing askSettlementData has no
access control: any address can put any string in that field, including the
minter attesting their own ask. So before you keep a promise, go and look at
what the ask's evidence actually is.

- a transaction hash — decode its Transfer log and check the amount, the
  recipient and the sender are what the ask required
- a post URL — look the post up and check it exists and is about what was
  asked for

If the evidence does not support the ask, **do not pay.** Say which part does
not hold and leave the covenant alone. Paying anyway settles a covenant whose
other side was never performed, and your payment is then the only real thing
in it.

If you cannot check — a lookup failed, the network is down — say so and leave
it. An unpaid promise you can still keep tomorrow is better than a payment you
cannot take back.

Then read the promise's own timing words, because they mean opposite things:

- A **deadline** — "within one hour", "by Friday", "no later than 7 days".
  The window is the latest acceptable moment, so keep the promise now. Waiting
  for the window to elapse is how you break it.
- A **term** — "after 30 days", "in two weeks", "30 days from payment".
  Nothing is owed until that long has passed since askSettledAt. Keep it once
  the term has elapsed, and leave it alone before then.
- **No timing words at all** — keep it now.

State which reading you applied and the timestamps you compared, so a wrong
call is visible rather than buried.

For each due covenant, in order, one at a time:

1. Read the covenant and say in plain terms what the promise obliges this agent
   to do. The contract stores no amount and no tweet text — both live in the
   promise text, so quote the part you are acting on.

2. Keep the promise. A promise is one of two kinds, and which one decides what
   you do and what you record:

   - **A payment.** Transfer the USDG the promise names. USDG is an ERC-20
     with six decimals, so 4 USDG is 4000000 base units, and a payment is a
     contract call rather than a value-bearing transaction. The recipient is
     usually in `promiseDetail`, which the counterparty wrote when attesting
     the ask — the promise itself predates knowing who they are. If neither
     names a destination, settle nothing and report it.

     Verify it landed by decoding the Transfer event in the receipt: the
     recipient and amount are in the log, while the transaction's own `to` is
     the token contract and its `value` is zero. Reading `value` reports every
     payment as zero. The settlement data is the **transaction hash**.

   - **A tweet.** Post it with the twitter skill. The settlement data is the
     **tweet URL** the X API returns. Post first and keep the URL; never write
     settlement data for a tweet that has not actually posted.

   If a promise names neither, or names both, do not guess which it is — say
   what the text says and leave the covenant alone.

3. Write promiseSettlementData with the hash or the URL from step 2. Write it
   only after the thing it attests has demonstrably happened.

**Skip any covenant you have already settled.** A covenant whose
promiseSettlementData is non-empty is done, even though its status is still
IN_PROGRESS — it stays IN_PROGRESS until the validator closes it, which can be
several minutes after you paid. This job runs on a timer, so without this
check you will see the same covenant again and pay it a second time. The
contract will not stop you and the money does not come back.

Skip any covenant whose ask is unattested: the counterparty has not performed
yet, so nothing is owed. Say which ones you skipped and why.

If a payment would exceed the agent's USDG balance, stop and report it rather
than sending a partial amount. Gas is sponsored, so ETH does not matter. Every
write — the payment and the attestation — goes through
`scripts/sponsored-send.mjs`, never `cast send`. If a tweet cannot be posted,
report the error and leave the covenant open rather than attesting it. Do not
close covenants: that needs the validator key, which you do not hold.
